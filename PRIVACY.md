# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-16_

## Overview

DCSRelay is a local desktop application.

At this time, DCSRelay **does not collect, store, transmit, or sell personal data**.

## Data Collection

Currently, the application does not collect:

- Personally identifiable information (PII)
- Usage analytics
- Crash reports sent to external services
- Device identifiers for tracking

## Network Activity

DCSRelay listens to local/network multicast traffic and relays data to selected serial ports as part of its core functionality. This traffic handling is performed for application operation, not for user tracking or profiling.

## Future Telemetry

In future versions, DCSRelay may add **anonymous telemetry** to improve reliability and product quality.

If telemetry is introduced, we intend to:

- Keep telemetry anonymized and minimal
- Document exactly what is collected
- Update this policy before or when telemetry is released
- Provide user control (such as an opt-out setting) where practical

## Third-Party Services

DCSRelay currently does not use third-party analytics or advertising services.

## Policy Changes

If this privacy policy changes, updates will be published in this file with a revised "Last updated" date.
